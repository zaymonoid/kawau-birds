North Island brown kiwi

kiwi-nui · Apteryx mantelli

Roosts in shady gullies and forages in the forest after dark. Listen for the males' shrill whistles.

Story

Kiwi have lived on Kawau for more than 140 years, yet numbers stayed puzzlingly low. Ponui Island, a little smaller, holds 1,500–2,000 kiwi descended from just 14 birds released in 1964.

Behaviour

Nocturnal and more often heard than seen. Kiwi probe leaf litter and soil with long bills that have the nostrils at the tip, so they smell their prey. Only the male incubates the egg.

Did you know? Kiwi lay one of the largest eggs of any bird relative to body size: about 124 mm long, from a bird weighing only 2–2.7 kg.
