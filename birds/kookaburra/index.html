Laughing kookaburra

Dacelo novaeguineae

Usually perched in tall trees at bush edges, and loudest at dawn and dusk.

Story

Grey released kookaburras on Kawau in 1866. Releases in Wellington, Nelson and Otago all failed, so New Zealand's wild kookaburras come from this island's birds.

Behaviour

Lives in family groups that roost together in a tall tree and defend a territory. A perch-and-pounce hunter, it waits motionless, drops onto a lizard, mouse or insect, then beats it against a branch and swallows it whole.

Did you know? In Australia snakes and reptiles make up about a third of its diet. New Zealand has no snakes, which may help explain why it has never spread far.
