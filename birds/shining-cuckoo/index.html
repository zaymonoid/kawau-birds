Shining cuckoo

pīpīwharauroa · Chrysococcyx lucidus

Found wherever grey warblers breed. Follow the whistles from September.

Story

A summer migrant that spends the winter in the Solomon Islands and the Bismarck Archipelago off New Guinea. The New Zealand subspecies breeds only here, arriving in September or October.

Behaviour

Lays single eggs in grey warbler nests, mostly in November, and leaves the warblers to raise the chick. Small and green, it hides in foliage and is heard far more than seen: a string of rising whistles.

Did you know? It eats hairy caterpillars that most birds avoid, and is one of the few New Zealand birds known to eat monarch butterfly caterpillars.

There’s more about this bird on Kawau in the island’s history.
