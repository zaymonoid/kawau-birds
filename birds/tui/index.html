Tūī

Prosthemadera novaeseelandiae

Commonly seen in every bay, feeding on flax, pōhutukawa and garden flowers.

Story

Tūī numbers rose on Kawau as possum control took hold in the 1990s. Today, locals say you can hear them around every bay and cove on the island.

Behaviour

Aggressive honeyeaters that will defend a flowering tree against every other bird. Their song mixes bell notes with coughs, clicks and wheezes, and they can mimic bellbirds and even phone ringtones.

Did you know? The tūī's loud whirring flight comes from a notch in its eighth primary feather. Some tūī fly more than 10 km a day to reach good nectar.
