Introduced

Indian peafowl

pīkao · Pavo cristatus

Commonly seen on the lawns, orchard and courtyards around Mansion House.

Story

Grey's peafowl had died out by 1923. Today's birds descend from about 20 released between 1958 and 1979, plus two from Motukiore Island around 1989.

Behaviour

Peafowl fly up to roost about 12 minutes after sunset and come down after dawn. In the display season each peacock keeps his own patch around Mansion House, out of sight of his rivals but within earshot.

Did you know? A Kawau peachick was once seen climbing to its roost using its feet and the claws on its wings, with its mother following it up.
