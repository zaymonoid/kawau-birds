Introduced

European greenfinch

Chloris chloris

Found in pines, gardens and along bush edges.

Story

Released in Nelson, Canterbury, Auckland and Otago from the early 1860s. It multiplied so fast that it was called the 'farmer's greatest enemy when grain is ripening'. Numbers fell as pasture replaced grain.

Behaviour

The stockiest introduced finch, with a heavy bill that cracks bigger seeds than other finches can. Winter flocks often mix with other finches, and one was estimated at 10,000 birds.

Did you know? One greenfinch was seen taking bees straight from a hive.
