Introduced

Mallard

rakiraki · Anas platyrhynchos

Commonly seen on ponds, dams and sheltered bays, though many are grey duck hybrids.

Story

Imported from English game farms from 1870, and later from Connecticut. Acclimatisation societies released over 30,000 until 1974. Mallards then hybridised with the native grey duck and have replaced it on most wetlands.

Behaviour

Dabbles and grazes on seeds, grasses and water plants, plus insects and snails. Pairs form each season, but the drake leaves all the incubating and duckling care to the female.

Did you know? Some New Zealand mallards have crossed the Tasman. Birds from Otago and Waikato have turned up in Adelaide, Lord Howe Island, New Caledonia and Vanuatu.
