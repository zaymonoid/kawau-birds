Long-tailed cuckoo

koekoeā · Urodynamis taitensis

Mostly passing through. Listen for harsh screeches, even at night.

Story

It breeds only in New Zealand but winters across an 11,000 km arc of Pacific islands, from Palau to the Pitcairn group. It is now threatened, partly because yellowheads, one of its host species, have declined.

Behaviour

A hawk-like cuckoo with a tail as long as its body, often mobbed by small birds. It lays in whitehead, brown creeper and yellowhead nests, and eats cicadas, wētā, lizards and other birds' nestlings.

Did you know? Young cuckoos leave for the tropics in April and May, months after the adults have gone. They make their first ocean crossing without their parents.
